# Privacy Policy for TintPro Restyle

**Last Updated:** September 13, 2026

Welcome to **BetterTintPro** ("the Extension"), a Google Chrome extension developed by Victor Hugo Del Carpio ("the Developer", "we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal data is handled in a safe and responsible manner. 

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected when you use BetterTintPro.

## 1. Information We Collect
**BetterTintPro does not collect, transmit, distribute, or sell your personal data.** 

Because the primary function of this extension is to provide local browser enhancements, the only data it handles is your extension preferences and settings:
* **Locally Stored Data:** The extension may use your browser's local storage (via the `chrome.storage` API) to save your configuration settings (such as color themes, intensity, or active/inactive status). 
* This data is kept locally on your device and/or synced directly via your personal Google account if Chrome Sync is enabled. It is never sent to the Developer or any third-party servers.

## 2. How We Use Your Information
Any configuration data saved by the Extension is used exclusively to:
* Remember your specific preferences for future browsing sessions.
* Ensure the Extension functions smoothly and as intended.

We do not have access to this data, meaning we do not use it for analytics, marketing, profiling, or tracking. 

## 3. Third-Party Access and Data Sharing
Since we do not collect any personal data or usage metrics, we do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to outside parties. 

**Google Chrome Web Store:** The Extension is distributed via the Google Chrome Web Store, which may collect basic, anonymized analytics (such as installation counts, operating system, and geographic region) in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The Developer only has access to aggregated, anonymized reports provided by the Chrome Web Store dashboard.

## 4. Required Permissions
BetterTintPro only requests the minimum permissions necessary to function properly on your browser. 
* Depending on its core features, it may require permissions to inject scripts on your active tabs (to apply the tints/visual changes). 
* The Extension will only read or modify the content of the web pages you visit to the extent required to provide its advertised functionality. It does not scrape, log, or monitor the content you view or the keystrokes you make.

## 5. Data Security
Because your preferences and settings are stored locally within your Chrome browser, data security is managed directly by your browser and your local machine. We encourage all users to keep their browsers up to date and to use strong security practices to protect their local devices.

## 6. Children’s Privacy
BetterTintPro is suitable for all ages. It does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13, nor from anyone else. 

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page, and the "Last Updated" date at the top will be modified accordingly. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any updates.
